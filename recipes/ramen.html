Quick Veggie Ramen Stir-Fry

This veggie ramen noodle stir-fry puts a delicious twist on fried rice by swapping in quick and easy ramen. Broccoli, onions, mushrooms, and egg are tossed with soy sauce-flavored noodles that fry up golden and crisp in the pan.

Ingredients

  • 2 (3 ounce) packages soy-flavored instant ramen noodles, such as Top Ramen(r), 1 season packet discarded, 1 saved
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup sliced button mushrooms
  • 1/3 cup chopped broccoli florets
  • 1 green onion, chopped white and green parts separated
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • Toasted sesame seeds, for garnish

Directions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add instant noodles and cook for 3 minutes. Drain noodles in a colander and run under cold water to stop the cooking. Shake off excess water.
  2. Meanwhile, heat sesame oil, mushrooms, broccoli, and white part of green onion in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook until browned, about 6 minutes. Turn heat off and set aside.
  3. Drizzle olive oil over noodles and add 1 soy seasoning packet from the instant noodle package. Shake until noodles are evenly coated.
  4. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add noodle mixture and for cook 4 minutes. Flip noodle mixture over and make a well in the center of noodles. Add egg, Cook until egg is set, about 4 minutes more.
  5. Using a spatula, chop up the egg. Add broccoli-mushroom mixture and stir until everything is combined. Cook 1 minute more.
  6. Transfer all to a serving dish and sprinkle with green part of green onions and toasted sesame seeds.
